## 1. Executive Summary & Data Source Investigation

In accordance with scientific integrity standards, the simulated NWP approximation (`obs_val * bias_ratio + noise`) has been decommissioned from all production workflows. VARSHAAI now directly ingests genuine Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP) precipitation guidance and pairs it with IMD ground truth observations.

## 2. Spatial Mapping & District Integration

NWP forecast grid points (0.25° $\approx$ 27 km resolution) are mapped to Indian district centroids using geographic coordinates (`latitude`, `longitude`, `elevation_m` from `INDIA_DISTRICT_MASTER`).

## 3. Temporal Alignment & Separation of Concepts

VARSHAAI enforces strict separation between three core concepts:
1. **`observed_rainfall_mm`**: True rainfall accumulated over 24 hours recorded at 08:30 IST by IMD ground observation stations.
2. **`raw_forecast_rainfall_mm`**: Uncalibrated raw numerical precipitation output from the NWP model initialized 24 hours ahead of valid time.
3. **`corrected_rainfall_mm`**: Post-processed rainfall prediction generated by the VARSHAAI regime-conditioned gradient boosting engine.

### Strict Temporal Timestamps
- `forecast_issue_time`: Forecast initialization (T-24h, 00:00 UTC / 05:30 IST).
- `forecast_valid_time`: Forecast valid timestamp (T, 08:30 IST).
- `lead_time_hours`: Exactly 24 hours (Day-1 lead).
- `observation_time`: Ground truth valid at 08:30 IST on the forecast valid day.
